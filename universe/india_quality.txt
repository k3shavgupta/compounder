# India quality list — the durability half of the gate, maintained by hand.
#
# WHY THIS IS MANUAL
# yfinance serves 4 years of Indian financials. Rules 2, 3 and 8 need 10, 6 and
# 6 years respectively, so they cannot be computed — and those are the rules
# doing most of the work in the US gate (69% and 31% kill rates). A 4-year
# automated gate would look like the US one and mean something much weaker, so
# the durability half stays human until better data exists.
#
# HOW TO REFRESH (quarterly is plenty — fundamentals move four times a year)
# Paste this into the query box at screener.in/screen/new/ and copy the symbols
# that come back into the list below.
#
#   Market Capitalization > 5000 AND
#   Return on capital employed > 15 AND
#   Average return on capital employed 5Years > 15 AND
#   Debt to equity < 0.5 AND
#   Interest Coverage Ratio > 5 AND
#   Promoter holding > 40 AND
#   Pledged percentage < 5
#
# Screener uses ROCE (pre-tax) where this project uses ROIC (post-tax). Close
# enough as a first pass; it flatters companies with low effective tax rates.
#
# Promoter holding and pledge have no US equivalent and are worth keeping — a
# mandated quarterly disclosure, and pledged promoter stock is one of the
# cheapest distress signals available anywhere.
#
# One ticker per line, .NS suffix, comments after #.
